# Privacy Policy

**Fatebook for Android** — Last updated: April 2026

## What data the app collects

- **API key**: Your Fatebook API key, used to authenticate with the Fatebook service. Stored locally on your device using Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256 encryption).
- **Prediction data**: Your questions, forecasts, and resolutions are fetched from Fatebook's servers and cached locally in an on-device database for offline access.
- **Notification preferences**: Your chosen reminder time and notification settings are stored locally on your device.

## What data the app does NOT collect

- No analytics or usage tracking
- No advertising or ad identifiers
- No crash reporting to third parties
- No personal information beyond what you provide to Fatebook

## Third-party services

This app communicates only with the [Fatebook API](https://fatebook.io) to sync your predictions. No other third-party services are contacted. Your API key is sent to Fatebook's servers as required for authentication — it is never sent anywhere else.

## Data storage

All data is stored locally on your device. The app does not back up data to cloud services (`android:allowBackup="false"`).

## Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data, including your API key and cached predictions.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/japancolorado/FatebookApp).
